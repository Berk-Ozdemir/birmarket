# Legal and distribution review

This document records release questions for the maintainer. It is not legal advice or a clearance opinion.

Before accepting real orders, confirm:

- The named MIT copyright holder has the rights to license all project-authored code, copy, design, product photography, fonts, and other assets.
- Any third-party assets and dependencies retain their original attribution and license terms.
- iyzico, PayTR, KargoJet, and email-provider merchant agreements permit the intended use and data flow.
- Customer notices, retention, deletion, consumer terms, returns/refunds, pricing, and shipping disclosures have been reviewed for the intended markets by qualified counsel.
- The operator's Azure, DNS, sender-domain, and data-backup configuration matches the published privacy and deployment descriptions.

Public source publication is separate from live commerce, a tagged software release, or distribution of customer data and credentials. The maintainer should complete and record this review before enabling live transactions.
